# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026

MiniZi ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the MiniZi mobile application.

### 1. Information Stored on Your Device
Most data generated by MiniZi—including study progress, quiz results, and user preferences—is stored locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the core features of the App.

### 2. Online & Multiplayer Features
If you choose to participate in online features or multiplayer games, minimal non-personally identifiable gameplay data (such as a chosen display name, avatar, and game score) may be transmitted to our servers to facilitate matchmaking and leaderboard rankings.

### 3. Purchases & Subscriptions
If you purchase an in-app product or subscription, all payment processing is handled directly and securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. MiniZi does not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial details.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The App may use standard third-party service providers to support core features:
* Cloud infrastructure for multiplayer connectivity and backend security.
* On-device tools for handwriting recognition and speech synthesis.
These services handle technical and diagnostic information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

### 5. Children's Privacy
MiniZi does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Where applicable, age verification is implemented to help ensure an age-appropriate experience.

### 6. Data Deletion & Inquiries
You can delete your locally stored data at any time by clearing the App data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. To request the removal of any online gameplay data or if you have questions about this policy, please contact us at:
* **Email:** [minisgimis@gmail.com](mailto:minisgimis@gmail.com)

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
